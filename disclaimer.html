3. Text & Translation Accuracy Disclaimer

The text of the Book of Enoch (1 Enoch) presented within Scrolls for Sol: Enoch is sourced from R.H. Charles' 1917 English translation, published by the Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge (SPCK), London, which is in the public domain.

While every reasonable effort has been made to present this text accurately and faithfully:

  • The R.H. Charles translation is one of many scholarly translations of 1 Enoch. Other translations (e.g., Nickelsburg, Knibb, Isaac) may differ significantly in wording, interpretation, and textual critical decisions.
  • Ge'ez transliterations provided in the App are approximate romanisations of Classical Ethiopic terms and names. They may not reflect all dialectal variations, scholarly conventions, or alternative transliteration systems.
  • Interpretive categories within the Watchers Explorer (including references to alien encounters, extraterrestrial phenomena, cosmic beings, and otherworldly entities) are speculative and presented for entertainment and educational exploration. They do not represent scholarly consensus, peer-reviewed research, theological doctrine, or historical fact.
  • MidMightBit Games does not guarantee textual accuracy, translational fidelity, or scholarly correctness of any text, transliteration, cross-reference, timeline, relationship graph, or interpretive content presented in the application.
  • The application is NOT endorsed by the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, any academic institution, any theological seminary, any scholarly body, or any religious authority of any denomination.
  • Cross-references, timelines, and the Watcher relationship graph represent editorial interpretations and should not be treated as authoritative scholarship.
  • Community annotations (SGT-gated) reflect the opinions and interpretations of individual contributors and are not endorsed by MidMightBit Games.

Guidance: For authoritative texts and scholarly analysis, we recommend consulting published academic editions, peer-reviewed scholarship, and qualified scholars of Ethiopic literature, Second Temple Judaism, or comparative religion.
