Data Source Attribution

Primary Text Source: R.H. Charles, The Book of Enoch or 1 Enoch, translated from the Ethiopic. Published by the Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge (SPCK), London, 1917. This translation is in the public domain.

The Book of Enoch (1 Enoch) is an ancient Jewish religious work, traditionally ascribed to Enoch, the great-grandfather of Noah. The text survives in its entirety only in the Ge'ez language (Classical Ethiopic). R.H. Charles' 1917 English translation is one of the most widely referenced scholarly translations and has been in the public domain for over a century.

While the underlying text is in the public domain, the specific compilation, arrangement, formatting, interpretive categories, Watchers Explorer content, reading plans, timeline data, relationship graph data, Ge'ez transliterations, cross-references, and presentation within Scrolls for Sol: Enoch are subject to the application copyright stated above.
