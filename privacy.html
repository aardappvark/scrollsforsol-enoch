1. Introduction

Scrolls for Sol: Enoch ("the App") is an ancient text reader application for Android devices. The App provides access to all 108 chapters of the Book of Enoch (1 Enoch) in the R.H. Charles 1917 translation, along with reading plans, the Watchers Explorer, annotations, highlights, bookmarks, cross-references, a timeline, a Watcher relationship graph, Ge'ez transliteration, AR constellations, and community annotations. Scrolls for Sol: Enoch also offers optional Solana wallet connection for Seeker Genesis Token (SGT) verification via the Sign In With Solana (SIWS) protocol.

This Privacy Policy explains how Scrolls for Sol: Enoch handles information when you use our App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. As you will read below, Scrolls for Sol: Enoch is designed with a privacy-first architecture: we do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data. The only data that ever leaves your device is an anonymous, aggregate usage count (an event name only) and optional Solana wallet verification — neither identifies you.

This policy applies to all users of the Scrolls for Sol: Enoch application, regardless of location or jurisdiction.

5. Network Requests & Third-Party APIs

Scrolls for Sol: Enoch makes minimal network requests, limited to the following:

5.1 Solana RPC (Wallet Verification Only)

Endpoint: api.mainnet-beta.solana.com
Purpose: Verify Seeker Genesis Token (SGT) ownership when you optionally connect a Solana wallet
Data sent: Wallet public address only (no PII)
When: Only when you explicitly initiate wallet connection via SIWS

A Solana wallet public address is a pseudonymous blockchain identifier. It is not linked to your real-world identity within our App. We do not store, log, or transmit this address to any server we control. The RPC request is made directly from your device to the Solana network.

5.2 Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA)

Wallet connection uses the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter protocol with Sign In With Solana (SIWS). This communication occurs locally between the App and your installed wallet app (e.g., Solflare, Phantom) via Android intents. No data is sent to MidMightBit Games servers.

5.3 No Other Network Requests

Aside from the optional Solana RPC and the anonymous analytics endpoint described in Section 6, Scrolls for Sol: Enoch makes no other network requests. There are no advertising networks, crash reporting services, remote configuration servers, or third-party analytics SDKs.

6. Anonymous Analytics, No Accounts, No Advertising, No Cross-App Tracking

Anonymous usage analytics. To understand overall usage and reach, the App sends a small number of anonymous, aggregate events to our own endpoint hosted on Vercel (Vercel Serverless Functions with Vercel Blob storage). When something happens in the App — for example the app is opened, the disclaimer is accepted, a chapter is read, or a wallet is connected — the App sends only the name of that event, from a fixed, limited list. It sends nothing else: never your wallet address, a device or user identifier, your bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading position, or any content. Vercel’s network derives an approximate country from the connection at the edge; your IP address is never logged, stored, or processed by us, and no city- or street-level location is collected. Events are kept only as aggregate counts (per event, per country, per day) and cannot be traced back to you or any device. The totals are publicly viewable at scrollsforsol-enoch-analytics.vercel.app/public-stats. Because the data is anonymous and aggregate and no IP is retained, it is not personal data under GDPR (Recital 26). You can turn it off at any time in the App’s Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics.

  • No Accounts: Scrolls for Sol: Enoch does not require or offer user account creation. There is no registration, login, or authentication system (apart from optional wallet verification via SIWS).
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any other third-party analytics SDK. Our only analytics is the first-party anonymous aggregate counter described above.
  • No Advertising: Scrolls for Sol: Enoch contains no advertisements. We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, ad networks, or mediation platforms. There are no ad identifiers collected or shared.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or any other tracking technology. We do not build user profiles or track behaviour across apps or websites.

8. Automated Decision-Making

Scrolls for Sol: Enoch performs one form of automated decision-making: sanctions compliance screening.

8.1 How Sanctions Screening Works

When the App starts, it may check your device's SIM card country, mobile carrier information, and/or system locale settings to determine whether you are located in or associated with a sanctioned jurisdiction. This check is:

  • Performed entirely on-device — no data is sent to any external server
  • Privacy-preserving — the raw location/carrier/locale data is compared locally against a static list of sanctioned regions and immediately discarded
  • Non-identifying — only a boolean result (permitted or blocked) is retained

If the screening determines that you are in a sanctioned region, access to the App will be restricted. This is required for compliance with Australian sanctions law, US OFAC regulations, and EU restrictive measures.

8.2 No Profiling

Scrolls for Sol: Enoch does not engage in any profiling, behavioural analysis, automated scoring, or algorithmic decision-making beyond the sanctions screening described above.

11. Data Security

Although Scrolls for Sol: Enoch does not transmit personal data, we take reasonable measures to protect locally stored information:

  • Encrypted wallet data: Any wallet verification status is stored using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences, which employs AES-256 encryption via the Android Keystore system.
  • Standard local storage: Non-sensitive preferences (reading progress, settings, annotations) use Android's standard SharedPreferences, protected by the Android application sandbox which prevents other apps from accessing this data.
  • Minimal remote attack surface: We store no personal data remotely; our analytics keeps only anonymous aggregate counts, so there is no personal database to breach.
  • Minimal data principle: We store only what is functionally necessary for the App to operate as expected.
