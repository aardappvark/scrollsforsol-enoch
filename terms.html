3. Description of Service

Scrolls for Sol: Enoch is an ancient text reader application designed for Android devices. The App provides:

  • All 108 chapters of the Book of Enoch (1 Enoch) in the R.H. Charles 1917 translation
  • Bookmarks, highlights, annotations, and favourites for personal study
  • Reading plans with curated thematic journeys through the text
  • Watchers Explorer with thematic categories for exploring fallen angels, cosmic journeys, and otherworldly beings
  • Timeline of events described in the Book of Enoch
  • Watcher relationship graph visualising angelic hierarchies and connections
  • Ge'ez transliteration of key terms and names
  • AR constellations feature overlaying astronomical references from the text onto the camera view
  • Community annotations (SGT-gated) for shared scholarly and reader notes
  • Cross-references linking related passages within the text
  • Verse of the day home screen widget
  • Text-to-speech for listening to chapters
  • Night mode for comfortable reading
  • Daily reading streak tracking
  • Optional Solana wallet connection for Saga Genesis Token (SGT) verification

The App is an educational and entertainment tool intended to provide access to an ancient text for personal study and exploration. It is not a substitute for scholarly commentary, theological instruction, or pastoral guidance.
